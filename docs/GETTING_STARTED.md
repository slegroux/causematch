# Getting started with Causematch

How to sign up and use Causematch as a volunteer, as a nonprofit coordinator, and as an admin. It is written for the live site, `https://causematch-vseu3pmeua-uw.a.run.app`; every path below (such as `/login`) goes after that address. Button and page names are in **bold**, exactly as the app shows them.

Contents: [Before you start](#before-you-start) · [Volunteer](#volunteer) · [Nonprofit coordinator](#nonprofit-coordinator) · [Admin](#admin) · [The preview](#rehearse-on-the-preview) · [Try the whole loop yourself](#try-the-whole-loop-yourself-in-15-minutes)

## Before you start

- **One email address, one role.** An address is a volunteer's, a coordinator's or an admin's, never two of them. An admin's address can't become a coordinator's, and a coordinator's or admin's address never signs up as a volunteer. To try all three roles you need **three different mailboxes**. A `+tag` of an address, or a Gmail address with its dots moved, counts as the same mailbox: a `+tag` of a coordinator's or admin's address can't sign up as a volunteer, and nothing is sent to it.
- **No password, no phone number.** You sign in with a link Causematch emails you. Nobody is asked for a phone number.
- **Links work once and expire after 15 minutes.** Only the newest link works: asking again cancels the one before.
- **The email comes from the Causematch address.** The subject is "Your Causematch sign-in link", or "Finish signing up for Causematch" the first time a volunteer signs up.
- **One browser holds one sign-in.** Signing in to a second role in the same browser signs the first one out. To try several roles, use a different browser (or a private window) for each.
- **Ask for the link in the browser you'll use.** If you open it in another browser or device, the confirm page asks "Did you ask for this link on another device? Then carry on. If someone sent it to you, don't." If you asked for it yourself, carry on.
- **Google.** If a **Continue with Google** button is shown, you can use it instead of the emailed link.

### If the link doesn't arrive

1. Wait a couple of minutes, then check your spam and Promotions folders.
2. Check that you typed the address exactly. The page answers the same thing for every address, so it can't tell you about a typo.
3. Ask again with **Send it again** (volunteers) or **Email me a link**. Each address can get at most 5 links an hour from one device and 5 a day in all, on both sign-in pages together. Past that you see **Too many tries**: wait, up to an hour, or try again the next day.
4. A coordinator gets a link only once an admin has approved their request.

## Volunteer

### Sign up

1. Open `/login` ("Sign in"). Type your email and tap **Email me a link**.
2. You see **Check your email**. Open the email and tap the link.
3. A page titled **Sign in to Causematch** says **Sign in as** and your address. Tap **Sign in**.
4. The first time, you land on onboarding, **Welcome to Causematch**.

### Onboarding

The page asks, in this order:

1. **About you:** your **First name**.
2. **Where:** **Neighborhood or ZIP** (in Los Angeles County), or **Use my location** if your browser offers it. Then **Getting there** (**Car**, **Transit**, **Bike** or **Walk**) and **Up to (minutes)** (15, 30, 45, 60 or **Any**). Only you see where you are. Nonprofits you like see only the neighborhood, which onboarding fills in from this place: a listed neighborhood as you picked it, a ZIP's neighborhood, and nothing for **Use my location**. You can change or clear it later in **Your profile**.
3. **What:** for each cause (Food insecurity, Environment & parks, Animal shelters, Homelessness services) pick **Love**, **Open to it** or **Not for me**. Pick at least one Love or Open to it. **Not for me** hides that cause's openings. Below the causes, tick **I need proof of my volunteer hours** if you need them for school, court or a work program: only nonprofits that confirm hours are shown, and you can get a certificate. Ticking it needs your **Last name**, because a certificate carries your full name.
4. Tick **I'm 18 or older and I agree to the privacy policy**, then tap **Start swiping**.

Your free times, the kind of work and the rest can be set later under **Me**, **Preferences**, one page with one **Save**.

### The deck (Openings)

The bottom bar has three tabs: **Openings**, **Matches** and **Me**.

- **Like** (the heart) or **Pass** (the cross) on each card. You can also drag the card.
- Tap the card, or **More**, to open **What you'll do**: the full description, "Good to know", any orientation, and any screening the nonprofit asks for. **Pass** and **Like** are there too.
- **Next 48h** (top right) shows only openings starting within 48 hours, soonest first.
- The **Filters** button (sliders and the word), left of Pass, opens **Filters**: travel time, which causes to show, and **Only this week**. Tap **Show openings**. Filters hold until you tap **Clear** and change none of your saved preferences.
- If you ticked **I need proof of my volunteer hours** and no nonprofit near you confirms hours yet, the deck reads "No nonprofit near you confirms hours yet". **Also show nonprofits that can't confirm hours (N)** shows them, and turns the proof-of-hours choice off in your preferences, so hours with them won't go on a certificate.
- A card marked **Screening required** means the nonprofit runs its own screening (a background check, Live Scan, a training or other) before your first shift.

### After a Like

1. The deck says "Liked! When (the nonprofit) says yes, it's a match. We'll email you."
2. After your first Like only, **Liked! Two quick questions** asks whether you volunteered in the last 12 months and how you heard about Causematch. Both are optional: **Save** or **Skip**.
3. On **Matches**, under **Your likes**, the like shows **Waiting for the nonprofit**.
4. When the nonprofit says yes, you get an email ("You're matched with ...") and **It's a match!** appears in the app. Tap **Say hi** to open the chat. If it says no, the like moves to **Past** as **Not this time**.
5. **Screening required openings:** you're matched and can chat, but no shift is booked yet. Matches shows "Waiting for (the nonprofit) to confirm your screening". The nonprofit confirms its own screening off the app, then confirms it in Causematch, which books your first shift. A nonprofit that has confirmed you once doesn't hold you again.

### Matches and chat

- Each match has a **Chat** button. Type in **Write a message** and tap send. When the nonprofit writes and you're not in the chat, you get an email "New message from ...".
- **More** on a match card has **Can't make this one** (cancel one shift), **Withdraw**, **Report** and **Block**.

### Shifts, hours and certificates

1. **Me**, then **Shifts and hours** (also a link at the top of Matches) lists **Upcoming** and **Past** shifts.
2. After a shift starts, it reads **Waiting for the nonprofit to mark you present**. Once the nonprofit marks it, it reads **Attended** with the hours confirmed.
3. Confirmed hours show under **Hours**, by month. Under **Certificates**, pick a month or **All my hours here** and tap **Get a certificate**. A certificate carries your full name, so add your **Last name** on **Your profile** first.
4. The certificate page has a check link (`/check/...`) that anyone can open to see it is genuine, a **Download PDF** button, and **Withdraw this certificate**.

### Me

- **Your profile** ("What nonprofits see"): your photo, **First name**, **Last name** (optional, unless **I need proof of my volunteer hours** is on: then it must stay filled in), pronouns, a short bio, **Neighborhood nonprofits see** (onboarding fills it in; change or clear it here), interests, and, under **Only we see**, your **Email (optional)** and other causes. Tap **Save profile**. Match news, reminders and last-minute calls go to the email here. Leave it empty and Causematch can't reach you outside the app.
- **Preferences** ("What you see, and when"): one page with one **Save**. It holds **Proof of hours** (**I need proof of my volunteer hours**, first on the page, with your **Last name**), **Causes and places**, **When**, **Kind of work** and **Last-minute calls** (off unless you turn it on).
- **Screenings** ("Confirmed by nonprofits"): which nonprofits confirmed your screening, its kind and the date. Causematch never sees documents or results.
- **Delete my account**: at the bottom of **Your profile**, then **Delete my account** on the confirm page. It erases your name, email and location, withdraws your matches, cancels your shifts and replaces your chat messages with "[message removed]". It can't be undone.
- **Sign out** is the last row of **Me**.

## Nonprofit coordinator

### Ask for access

Nobody gets a nonprofit account by signing up: you ask, and an admin approves.

1. Open `/org/claim` ("Claim your organization").
2. Under **Find your organization**, start typing its name and pick it from the IRS records. That fills in **Organization's EIN** and **Organization name**. You can also type the EIN.
3. Fill in **Your name** and **Your email**, the address you'll sign in with.
4. Tick **I'm authorized to represent this organization** and tap **Request access**.
5. You see **Request received**: "If it is approved, we'll email the address you gave with where to sign in." No email comes now. Causematch takes only nonprofits in Los Angeles County whose work fits one of its causes.

### Sign in after approval

1. You get an email, "You're approved on Causematch". It has **no sign-in link**, only where to sign in: `/org/login`. You ask for the link there next.
2. Open `/org/login` ("Nonprofit sign in"), type your email and tap **Email me a link**.
3. Open "Your Causematch sign-in link", tap the link, then **Sign in** on the confirm page. You land on **Your openings**.
4. The first time, the page asks you to "Add your name so volunteers know who you are". Type your name and role and tap **Save details**. **Post an opening** appears after that.

The tabs along the top are **Openings**, **Inbox**, **Shifts** and **Organization** (your logo, photo, description, website and **We can confirm volunteer hours**).

### Post an opening

1. Tap **Post an opening**.
2. **The opening:** a title, **What will volunteers do?**, and the **Cause**.
3. **Where and when:** the **ZIP code** (Los Angeles County only; the **Neighborhood** fills in from it), the **Date and time of the next shift** (Los Angeles time, in the future, within 12 months), **How long is a shift?**, and **Does it repeat?** (**One-time**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Flexible / ongoing**).
4. **Volunteers:** how many you need (1 to 200), whether an orientation is required, and anything volunteers should know.
5. **Screening (optional):** tick **Volunteers must clear our screening before their first shift** and pick **Which screening?** (**Background check**, **Live Scan fingerprinting**, **Training** or **Other**). You run the screening yourself; Causematch only records that you confirmed it.
6. **The adults rule (required):** tick the box that confirms the opening is for adults (18+). Without screening it also confirms there is no one-to-one contact with vulnerable people; with screening it says you screen each volunteer before their first shift. Homelessness services openings are for group shifts only, unless they require your screening.
7. **More details (optional)** holds the kind of work, languages and skills. Tap **Post opening**.

A one-time opening leaves volunteers' decks when its date and time arrive, and a volunteer accepted after that gets no shift booked: schedule one for them (see Shifts). A weekly opening stays: once its first date has passed, it shows the same weekday and time next week, and the shift an accepted volunteer gets is booked on that date.

**Close an opening.** Under **More**, **Close opening** works on an open or a filled opening. A page first names the volunteers with an upcoming shift (or says "Nobody has an upcoming shift on it."). Closing cancels their upcoming shifts, and each of them gets a message in their chat ("(Nonprofit) closed this opening, so your shift on (date) is cancelled.") and the usual new-message email. The opening leaves volunteers' decks; past shifts and their hours stay. **Reopen** brings a closed opening back while its date is still ahead, but the cancelled shifts stay cancelled.

**Weekly openings, the limits.** Spots don't reset each week: a filled weekly opening stays filled from week to week, like a standing team, until someone leaves. Only the first shift is booked automatically (see Shifts). A closed weekly opening whose first date has passed can't be reopened: post a new one.

**Last-minute calls.** When an opening starts within 48 hours and still has spots, its **More** menu offers **Short of people? Post a last-minute call** (not on screening-required openings, and up to 3 a day). Nearby volunteers who turned on last-minute calls see it first. The emails to them go out only once the site's scheduled jobs are running (an operator step).

### The inbox: accept or decline

1. When a volunteer likes an opening you get an email, "New volunteer interest on Causematch". Open **Inbox**.
2. Under **Waiting for you**, each volunteer shows their first name and last initial, what they're interested in, the shift, and what they shared.
3. Tap **Accept**, or **Decline**. A decline can be undone for 2 minutes (**Undo**); the volunteer isn't told until then.
4. Accepting books the volunteer's first shift at the opening's date and time, and opens the chat: tap **Say hi** in the green banner, or **Chat** on their card.
5. **Screening required:** an accepted volunteer shows **Screening pending**. Once your own screening is done, tap **Confirm screening**, then **Confirm** on the next page. That books their first shift and is remembered for your nonprofit.

### Shifts

- **Did they come?** lists shifts that have started. Check the hours (the list starts at the shift's length; pick another if they stayed longer or left early), then tap **Attended** or **No-show**. You can correct a mark once, within 7 days.
- **Upcoming** lists booked shifts, each with **Chat** and **Cancel this shift**.
- **Later shifts:** only the first shift is booked automatically. For a weekly opening, tap **Same time next week**. For any other date, open the volunteer's card in **Inbox**, tap **Schedule another shift**, pick the date and time and the length, and tap **Schedule**.

### Adding a second coordinator

There is no invite button. The second person files their own request at `/org/claim` with the same EIN, and an admin approves it (the admin ticks "Yes, add them too"). An operator can also add one directly; see [docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md](ADMIN_GUIDE.md#a-nonprofit-needs-a-second-coordinator).

## Admin

- **Who is an admin:** only the addresses the operator lists in the `ADMIN_EMAILS` setting when deploying. You can't sign up as one.
- **Signing in:** at `/org/login`, the nonprofit sign-in page, with **Email me a link**, as a coordinator does. You land on the **Admin** page.
- **The tabs:** **Admin**, **Metrics**, **Reports**, **Orgs**, **Claims** and **Blocks**. An admin sees only these pages: no deck, no openings, no chat.
- **The job that unblocks everyone:** approving claims. Nobody is told when a request arrives, so open **Claims** every day or two. Check each request, then tap **Approve** (or **Decline**). Approving also verifies the nonprofit, which puts its openings in volunteers' decks.
- **Metrics:** the **Metrics** tab, or the **Pilot metrics** box on the Admin page. **Download CSV** saves the figures.

Everything else (the checks before approving, reports, suspensions, blocks, reading the metrics) is in [docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md](ADMIN_GUIDE.md).

## Rehearse on the preview

The preview is a separate copy of Causematch filled with sample data, for trying the three roles before you use the live site. Open it with the preview link you were sent, and don't pass the link on.

- **Try it as a volunteer** on `/login` signs you in as Sam, a fresh copy for each tap, with matches, a chat, shifts and hours already confirmed, so a certificate is ready. Nonprofits there answer a like within a few seconds.
- **Try it as a nonprofit**, on `/login` or `/org/login`, signs you in as the sample coordinator, Pat.
- **Try it as an admin** on `/org/login` signs you in as the sample admin.

What is seeded: a nonprofit that confirms volunteer hours (its cards say **Confirms your hours**), an opening marked **Screening required**, a last-minute call for tomorrow, and a pending claim to approve under **Claims**.

What you do on the preview stays there. To put it back as it started, an admin taps **Reset demo data** on the **Admin** page, then **Reset demo data** on the confirm page. Every like, match, chat, shift and claim, and every account made on the preview, goes, and the sample data comes back with fresh dates. It can't be undone, and it signs you out: tap **Try it as an admin** to carry on. A reset signs out everyone on the preview, so don't press it while someone else is in the middle of a demo. A redeploy or restart of the preview does too, and brings the sample data back as a reset does. The preview can be reset once every 15 minutes in all, and 3 times an hour from one address; past either you see **Too many tries** and nothing is reset. If someone is mid-action during a reset, a stray notice from it can be left on the preview.

The live site has no sample data and no **Reset demo data**. It has no nonprofits until real ones ask for access and an admin approves them.

## Try the whole loop yourself in 15 minutes

You need three mailboxes (admin, coordinator, volunteer), one browser or private window for each, and a real Los Angeles County nonprofit you're allowed to represent: production has no test organization, and approving the claim makes that nonprofit visible to every volunteer. Ask the operator to list the admin address in `ADMIN_EMAILS` first.

The timing rule that matters: the opening must start in the future when you post it, the volunteer must like it and the coordinator accept it **before** it starts (that books the shift), and the shift can be marked only **once it has started**. So post it about 10 minutes ahead.

1. **Coordinator browser:** open `/org/claim`, find the nonprofit, fill in the coordinator's name and email, tick the box, tap **Request access**.
2. **Admin browser:** sign in at `/org/login`. Open **Claims**, find the request and tap **Approve** (tick any checkbox the card shows first). The banner says "Approved. We emailed them where to sign in."
3. **Coordinator browser:** sign in at `/org/login` with the coordinator address. Add your name and tap **Save details**.
4. Tap **Post an opening**. Note the ZIP code you use. Set **Date and time of the next shift** to about 10 minutes from now, **How long is a shift?** to 1 hour, **Does it repeat?** to **One-time**, 1 volunteer, orientation **No**. Leave screening unticked, tick the adults box, tap **Post opening**.
5. **Volunteer browser:** sign up at `/login` with the volunteer address. In onboarding, type the **same ZIP code** under **Neighborhood or ZIP**, leave **Car** and 30 minutes, and pick **Love** for the opening's cause. Tick the consent box and tap **Start swiping**.
6. Turn on **Next 48h** so the soonest openings come first. Find your opening and tap **Like**, then **Skip** the two questions.
7. **Coordinator browser:** open **Inbox** (an email "New volunteer interest on Causematch" also arrives). Tap **Accept**, then **Say hi** and send a message.
8. **Volunteer browser:** open **Matches**. The match shows **Matched**; tap **Chat** to read the message and reply. The email "You're matched with ..." arrives too.
9. **Wait until the opening's start time.** Before then the shift sits under **Upcoming** and can't be marked.
10. **Coordinator browser:** open **Shifts**. Under **Did they come?**, leave the hours at 1 hour and tap **Attended**.
11. **Volunteer browser:** open **Me**, **Shifts and hours**. The shift reads **Attended**, "1 hour confirmed", and **Hours** shows it. To try a certificate, add a **Last name** on **Your profile**, then tap **Get a certificate**.
12. **Admin browser:** open **Metrics** to see where the pilot's figures appear (with one volunteer, most rates read "too few").

Afterwards: the test opening leaves every deck once its start time has passed. Delete the test volunteer from **Me**, **Your profile**, **Delete my account**. Removing the coordinator or the admin needs the operator ([docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md](ADMIN_GUIDE.md#someone-wants-their-account-deleted)).
